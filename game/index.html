How to Play

  • Claim a start. On your first turn, pick any cell on the board.
  • Grow your territory. After that you can only reveal cells touching one of your own (including diagonals).
  • Peek & remember. Revealing a cell briefly shows the numbers around it — memorise them.
  • Build combos. Chaining the same parity (all odd or all even) grows your multiplier. Multiples of 5 stack a bigger bonus.
  • Win. When no moves remain, the higher score wins.
